A cartridge that renders wrong, a save that will not load, an addon you want written. This reaches a person, by email.

Message

The contact form is switched off on this server. Whoever runs it can turn it on by setting TINYBIRD_CONTACT_KEY in .env. In the meantime the repository's issue tracker is the way through.

Messages you have already sent, and whatever came back.

Sending a message needs an account — it is what the reply is addressed to, and what stops the form becoming a relay for whoever finds it. Sign in from the account menu, top right.

Ten characters or more, so there is something to answer.

The form key stays on the server, the same as the vault key. This page posts to it and it talks to the contact service. You receive a confirmation by email, then support answers both there and on your ticket. One message a minute, three an hour.

Before you write

Two of the most common reports already have answers on the info page: trading stops short of a Pokémon actually changing hands, and the stream overlay is switched off on purpose. Neither is a bug in your copy.

A ROM that renders wrong is worth reporting either way — say which cartridge and whether gba_bios.bin is in place, because a missing BIOS dump accounts for most of them.
